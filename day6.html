Key Lesson from Call 1

If they have zero itinerary ads after 90 days and are upset, let them cancel gracefully. The only partners worth saving are those with at least some traction (itinerary adds, favorites, referral clicks).

Which Conversation Thread to Use in Intercom

Always reply in the partner's most recent active conversation. If their last conversation was 3+ months ago with no recent activity, don't reply there — find or start the current one. Cancellation confirmations should go where the partner is most likely to see them.

Call Notes & Logging

Add Notes to Every Intercom Conversation

Each Intercom conversation must have a pinned note showing exactly where you are in the process. This prevents you from re-reading the entire thread every time. As conversations evolve (proactive outreach → cancellation request → churn call), update the note.

Intercom Conversation Note Format

  • Current status: What type of conversation is this right now? (Proactive outreach / Cancellation request / Churn follow-up / Optimization update)
  • Last action: What was the last thing done or sent?
  • Next step: What needs to happen next and when?
  • Signal level: Weak / Medium / Strong

Example: "Proactive outreach sent May 2. Partner replied requesting cancellation May 3. Churn call scheduled May 4. Offered monthly + $150 — declined. Scheduled cancellation May 19."

Stefan's Math — Capacity Analysis

Stefan broke down the 2,000 tickets across 3 agents to challenge the "understaffed" narrative:

  • 2,000 tasks / 3 agents = 666 tasks/month per agent
  • 666 / 4 weeks = 166 tasks/week per agent
  • 166 / 5 days = 33 tasks/day per agent
  • 33 tasks x 12 minutes average = 396 minutes = 6.6 hours/day
  • With 8-hour shift minus lunch/breaks = team appears adequately staffed on paper

The gap: this math excludes portal work (listing edits, QA, optimization) which has zero Airtable tracking. The team's actual per-task time is 20–25 minutes for optimization tasks, not 10–12. Luis acknowledged the portal tracking gap.

The Real Gap: Portal Work Is Invisible

Everything done inside the admin portal (listing edits, QA, optimization, tag updates, media arrangement) has zero productivity tracking. Luis confirmed this. Until this is solved, the team cannot fully prove capacity constraints with data. This is Rick's most important operational improvement to push for with Stefan and Luis.

⚠️ Time Zone Confusion — Always Use Central

HubSpot availability settings default to Eastern Time. If you set 10 AM in Eastern, it will show up as 9 AM Central on the booking page. Always explicitly set the time zone to Central Time (Chicago) in your HubSpot calendar settings, not just on your Google Calendar.

Why Two Calendar Profiles Matter

  • Let's Batch email profile: Used for all Batch-related tools — Airtable, HubSpot, Intercom, partner portal, Claude Batch
  • NewWork.co email profile: Used for all NeoWork tools — Google Drive, NeoWork Academy, team documents, Claude NewWork
  • Critical rule: Always add your newwork.co email as a guest on every calendar event created in Let's Batch — so both calendars stay in sync
  • Why it matters: If Batch ever offboards you and revokes the Let's Batch email access, everything you saved there disappears. Keeping newwork.co synced is your backup.
