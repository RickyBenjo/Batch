NeoWork Time Tracking & Infraction Handling

Why This Matters

The NeoWork timesheet is how teammates get paid and how attendance is verified. As supervisor, you are responsible for ensuring all teammate hours are accurate every week. Infractions appear in the NeoWork Slack bot and must be resolved promptly. Inaccurate records lead to payroll errors and incorrect attendance percentages in the MBR.

The Two Ways to Fix an Infraction

MethodWhen to UseLimitation
NeoWork Bot (Slack)Preferred for fixing clock-in/clock-out issues. Select the infraction type, add comment if needed, enter hour override, submit.Clock in/out fields become editable. Bot must send the infraction first before you can resolve it.
Airtable (direct edit)For fixing shift length, shift start/end times, adding external comments, and leave/day-off corrections.Cannot fix the "absent" status caused by missing clock in/out — that requires the NeoWork bot.

Common Scenarios & How to Fix Them

1
Teammate shows as "Absent" but actually worked (logged in outside grace period)Open infraction in NeoWork bot → select "Outside Grace Period, Default to Scheduled Shift" → this fixes the absent status and restores the correct clock in/out.
2
Teammate did coverage (extended shift)NeoWork bot → select "Activity" (not Overtime) → add comment: "Extended shift for X hours to cover for [teammate]" → enter total hours worked as the override (e.g., if regular shift 8 hrs + 4 hrs coverage = enter 12).
3
Teammate on approved leave but still showing a shift lengthOpen the record in Airtable → set shift start time to OFF and shift end time to OFF → set shift length to 0 → close. The external comment should already say "on leave vacation" or "mental health day."
4
Day off showing as shift with 8 hoursOpen the record in Airtable → shift start = OFF, shift end = OFF, shift length = 0. Remove any incorrect external comment. The record should show zero shift length for a day off.
5
Your own hours are wrongDo NOT edit your own hours in Airtable. Send the issue to Jelly at NeoWork to fix. Same rule for Cliff's hours.

Activity vs. Overtime — Key Difference

  • Activity (Coverage): A teammate voluntarily covers another's shift during their own working hours. Select "Activity" in the NeoWork bot. No premium pay — treated as regular hours. Add a comment explaining who was covered.
  • Overtime: Batch explicitly requests the team to work beyond contracted hours. Select "Overtime" in the bot. Triggers premium pay (typically 20–30% per hour, depending on the contract).
  • Key rule: Never select Overtime for coverage situations. Only use Overtime when Batch directly requests extra hours.

How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

  • Formula: Total Final Hours ÷ Shift Length = Attendance %
  • Quick tip: type the formula directly into the browser URL bar as a calculator (e.g., "36/36") and press Enter
  • A teammate showing 0 regular hours with a shift length present will show as "absent" — even if they actually worked. Always check both fields together.
  • Log-in grace period: teammates should log in within 15 minutes before their shift starts and log out within 15 minutes after shift end. Outside this window triggers an infraction.
  • If a teammate logs in and out twice (e.g., regular shift + coverage), NeoWork only tracks the first log in and first log out. Subsequent log in/out pairs are not tracked. This is why coverage is logged via the NeoWork bot override, not a second log in/out.

Correction 1 — Listing QA: Location Setting

Earlier notes said: "Location is always set to the partner's location."

Correct: Location depends on the experience type:

  • Fixed venue (bar, restaurant, activity space, boat): Set to partner's location with partner's address
  • Mobile or delivery experience (IV drips, custom bracelet-making, mobile bar): Set to "at home" or customer's location — the experience comes to the customer
  • Bar hopping / roving experience: Partner may define a meeting point — confirm with them before setting
  • When in doubt, check the experience description or ask the partner. Never assume — wrong location settings lead to customer complaints.

Correction 2 — Listing QA: Recently Live vs. Proactive Outreach Scope

Clarification: There are two different scopes depending on how you got the task:

  • Recently Live QA (from Airtable NW Listing QA Tracking view): QA only the specific experience listed in that Airtable entry. Do not proactively check the partner's other listings unless flagged.
  • Proactive Outreach (from Core Engagement Google Sheet): Check and optimize ALL listings that partner has — not just one. Do a full audit of their entire presence.
