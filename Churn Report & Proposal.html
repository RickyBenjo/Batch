Key insight

79% of firm cancellations cite no ROI or no value — problems that exist weeks before the cancellation call. These are not retention failures. They are engagement gaps that started at onboarding.

Why Reactive Calls Can't Fix This

How partners reach us

Partners don't receive outbound calls from the team. They book us through the chatbot when they want to cancel. By the time we hear from them, the decision is already made.

Step 1

Partner joins Batch

Onboarded, listing created. No follow-up contact after sign-up.

Step 2

Partner goes quiet

Weak signal, no bookings, incomplete listing. Still no one reaches out.

Step 3

Decision is made

Frustration builds. Partner decides to leave. Mind already made up.

Step 4 — Now

We get the call

Partner books through chatbot to cancel. 70% are unwinnable at this point.

The real issue

Partners book the call to cancel, not to be convinced. We're not losing them on the call — we're losing them in the silence after onboarding. The intervention window closes long before the chatbot opens.

What proactive outreach looks like

Target partners before they've made up their minds. At-risk signals we can act on:

  • 0 itinerary ads — no visibility, no traffic
  • No bookings in 60+ days — platform not working for them
  • Incomplete listing — partner page, no media, no reviews
  • No login activity — disengaged and drifting toward churn
  • Previous unanswered cancellation request

As Gaby and Aljun ramp up and take ownership of the cancellation queue, Rick and Kring transition to proactive outreach — reaching at-risk partners before they ever reach the chatbot.

Why this works for Gaby & Aljun's development

Cancellation calls are structured, repeatable, and have clear outcomes — the best kind of ramp activity. They build confidence on real calls, get direct feedback, and own meaningful work from day one. The queue stays covered while senior capacity moves to where it actually improves the save rate.
