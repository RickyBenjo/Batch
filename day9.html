Coverage vs. Overtime — Know the Difference

  • Coverage: A teammate volunteers or is assigned to cover another teammate's shift during their own working hours. Treated as regular hours — no premium pay. The covering teammate absorbs the work within their normal shift.
  • Overtime: Batch explicitly asks the team to work beyond contracted hours. This triggers a rate premium (typically +20–30% per hour depending on the contract). Batch initiates this, not the team.
  • Key rule: Always tag coverage properly in the roster so the record shows who covered what and when. Without this, coverage looks like extra hours and creates discrepancies in the NeoWork timesheet.

Important: Airtable Does Not Write Back to the Portal

Customer Disputes — Key Differences from Partner Disputes

Today's Case — Daniel Conn ($654 Dispute)

A dispute was filed for $654 — marked as "fraudulent." Investigation confirmed this is a customer (not a partner), who booked a Private Hibachi Chef Experience for an August 8 event date. The customer went through the full booking process themselves, adding their card details and confirming the booking. The dispute reason of "fraudulent" is inconsistent with having placed the booking themselves. This is most likely a case of buyer's remorse or a desire to cancel, not fraud.

How to Find if a Dispute Is from a Customer vs. a Partner

1
Copy the email from the Stripe disputeThe email address is always shown in the dispute details
2
Search in Airtable (vendor directory) firstIf they appear as a partner record, it's a partner dispute. If nothing shows up, it's likely a customer.
3
Check in Old BAT — Users sectionPaste the email into Old BAT → Users → All Users. If they appear there with party/booking history, they're a customer.
4
Click through to their booking detailsFind the specific order, confirm the charge amount, check the event date, and review the price breakdown including commission
5
Check if they have a future-dated bookingIf the event hasn't happened yet, a refund may be appropriate — subject to the cancellation policy (7-day notice for full refund)

Handling a Customer Dispute — Process

ScenarioAction
Customer claims fraud but clearly placed the booking themselvesCounter the dispute. The booking process itself is evidence of authorization (card entry, event selection, confirmation). Call the customer, explain the process, offer to cancel through proper channels.
Customer wants to cancel a future-dated bookingIf within the 7-day cancellation window, offer a full refund and ask them to withdraw the dispute. Process the refund through the proper channel, not through the dispute.
Customer is past the cancellation window but the event is in the futureOffer partial refund or credit per policy. Ask them to contact their bank to withdraw the dispute first before processing anything.
Customer was genuinely charged in errorAccept the dispute. Do not counter when the evidence clearly supports the customer's claim.

If they decline or are unreachable, counter the dispute with the booking authorization evidence.

Email Partners — How Payment Works

  • Email partners = customer pays Batch directly → Batch takes a commission (currently ~10%) → Batch transfers the remainder to the partner via Stripe Connect
  • The partner must have a Stripe Connect account set up and connected in Admin for transfers to work
  • If a partner has no Stripe Connect ID linked, transfers will not happen — this is a setup gap that needs fixing
  • Always verify that the Stripe Connect account is properly linked when doing listing QA for email or bookable partners
  • Commission was previously ~25%, now ~10% — this affects the business case for heavy manual fulfillment work per partner
