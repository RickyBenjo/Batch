Section 05
Root Cause — Why Reactive Calls Can't Fix This
Partners book us through the chatbot when they're ready to cancel. By the time we hear from them, the decision is already made.
Step 1
Partner joins Batch
Onboarded, listing created. No follow-up contact after sign-up.
Step 2
Partner goes quiet
Weak signal, no bookings, incomplete listing. Still no one reaches out.
Step 3
Decision is made
Frustration builds. Partner decides to leave. Mind already made up.
Step 4 — Now
We get the call
Partner books through chatbot to cancel. 70% are unwinnable at this point.
⚠️
The real issue
Multiple partners said nobody from Batch ever reached out after they signed up. For several, the cancellation call was the first time they spoke to anyone on the team. We're not losing them on the call — we're losing them in the silence after onboarding.

A phased plan that matches where the team actually is — progressively freeing senior capacity for proactive work as new specialists ramp up.
🎯

Shift Rick and Kring toward admin, proactive outreach, disputes, and past dues — the work that gets ahead of churn before it starts. Gaby and Aljun absorb the cancellation queue as they ramp, eventually taking on proactive outreach too.
