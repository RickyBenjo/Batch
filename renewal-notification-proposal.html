Pre-Renewal Notification System

A low-effort fix to reduce surprise cancellations and refund exposure on longer-term plans.

Submitted by Rick
For Stefan & Luis
Action Required

The Problem

We're getting more calls from partners on annual, 6-month, and 3-month plans who are surprised by renewal charges. Some were told they'd be notified before renewal. When that doesn't happen, they come to us already frustrated and in full cancel mode — making retention significantly harder and refund exposure much higher on larger commitments.

Revive Well Tahoe

Partner on a 12-month plan was assured by sales it wouldn't renew automatically. By the time they reached us, they just wanted out.

Lost Cat & Black Wood

Partner on a 6-month plan had no idea she'd be charged again at renewal on May 10. Now requesting cancellation and a refund for the remaining months.

Why This Matters

These aren't isolated cases. This pattern appears consistently in our churn call logs. Partners on longer commitments represent larger refund amounts at risk — and these situations are almost always avoidable with a simple heads-up.

Key Insight

Top churn reasons — no bookings/ROI, no perceived value, budget — are largely detectable before the call happens, with no post-onboarding contact. A pre-renewal touchpoint is one of the few interventions we can make before a partner reaches cancel mode.

The Fix

Automated Notification 14 Days Before Renewal

Send partners on annual, 6-month, and 3-month plans a notification 14 days before their renewal date. This aligns with our existing 14-day cancellation policy and gives partners a path to connect with us while they're still in a good headspace.

Near-Term Fix

Manual check-ins 7–14 days before renewal using a Stripe report already pulled to identify upcoming renewals by plan type.

Long-Term Goal

Automated notification system that runs without team intervention — reducing refund exposure on annual and 6-month plans at scale.

What We Need From You

Data Source

A Stripe report has been pulled to identify partners by plan type and upcoming renewal date. Happy to defer to a cleaner source on your end if one exists — Airtable, HubSpot, etc.

Automation Tool

Looking for your guidance on the right tool to trigger these notifications — whether that's through Intercom, an existing workflow, or something else entirely.

Bottom Line

Your go-ahead and a decision on tooling is all we need to get this moving. The partner list is ready on our end.
